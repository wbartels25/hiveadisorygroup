Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Hive Advisory Group ("Hive", "we", "us") respects the privacy of the people who visit hiveadvisorygroup.com and the business professionals we correspond with. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you request a briefing through our website, we collect your name, company, company size and business email address. When you email us, we receive the information contained in your message.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours uses analytics tools that collect standard technical information, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, the referring website and time spent on the site. These tools use cookies or similar technologies. We use:

  • Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag, to measure the reach of our LinkedIn activity and understand the professional audience visiting the site.
  • Apollo.io, to identify the companies whose employees visit the site, based on IP address. We use this information at the company level.

Business contact information

We may reach out to business professionals by email about our advisory services. That contact information comes from publicly available sources and business data providers. If you would prefer not to hear from us, a reply to any of our emails or a note to info@hiveadvisorygroup.com is all it takes, and we will stop.

How we use information

  • To respond to briefing requests and other inquiries.
  • To understand which topics and services are of interest and to improve the site.
  • To follow up with organizations that may benefit from our services.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

How information is shared

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and the business, including Netlify (website hosting and form processing), Google, LinkedIn, Apollo.io and Microsoft (email). These providers handle information under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Your choices

  • Most browsers allow cookies to be blocked or deleted.
  • Google offers a browser add-on that opts out of Google Analytics, available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • LinkedIn members can manage advertising preferences in their LinkedIn account settings.
  • You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to info@hiveadvisorygroup.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will respond to requests as those laws require.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as it is needed for the purposes described here, or as long as the law requires. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, although no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This site is intended for business audiences. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
